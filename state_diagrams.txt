PASSWORD – CONCEPTUAL PRODUCT DFA

Track:
L = length status
U = uppercase seen
l = lowercase seen
D = digit seen
S = special character seen

Accept only when:
8 <= length <= 16
AND U = 1
AND l = 1
AND D = 1
AND S = 1
AND no whitespace
